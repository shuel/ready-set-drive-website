Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Ready Set Drive respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you contact us through our website.

Who we are

Ready Set Drive is operated by Shuel Ahmed as a sole trader. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Shuel Ahmed is the data controller responsible for personal information collected through this website.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, you can contact us at hello@readysetdrive.co.uk .

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry through our website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile number
  • Your email address, if provided
  • Your area or postcode, if provided
  • Information you include in your enquiry message

We only ask you to provide information that is relevant to responding to your enquiry about our driving lessons or services.

How we use your information

We use information submitted through the website to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Discuss lesson availability and our services
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry
  • Keep appropriate business records where necessary

We do not sell your personal information or use information submitted through the enquiry form for unrelated advertising.

Our lawful basis for using your information

We normally process information submitted through our enquiry form because it is necessary to respond to a request you have made before potentially entering into an agreement for driving lessons.

In some circumstances, we may also process information where it is necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as maintaining appropriate records and protecting our business, provided those interests do not override your rights and interests.

How your enquiry is processed

Our website uses Web3Forms to process website enquiry form submissions. Information entered into the enquiry form is therefore transmitted through Web3Forms before being delivered to our Ready Set Drive business email account.

Our business email is provided through Zoho Mail. These service providers process information on our behalf in connection with providing the enquiry and email services we use.

How long we keep your information

If you make an enquiry but do not become a pupil, we normally keep your enquiry information for up to 12 months after our last contact with you. We may retain information for longer where there is a legitimate business or legal reason to do so.

If you become a pupil, information relating to your lessons and our business relationship may need to be retained for different periods for business, tax, legal or regulatory purposes.

Web3Forms may separately retain information processed through its service in accordance with its own data retention arrangements.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share personal information where necessary to operate our services, comply with a legal obligation, protect our legal rights, or where you have asked us to do so.

This may include service providers that help us operate the website, process enquiries and provide business email services.

International data transfers

Some of the service providers used to operate our website or communications may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this occurs, appropriate safeguards are required to protect personal information in accordance with applicable data protection law.

Cookies and website tracking

We do not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tracking technologies on the Ready Set Drive website.

If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, provide appropriate information and choices regarding cookies.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights over your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Ask us to delete your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your information in certain circumstances
  • Object to certain uses of your personal information
  • Ask for your information to be transferred where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@readysetdrive.co.uk .

Complaints

If you have concerns about how we use your personal information, please contact us first so that we can try to resolve the issue.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or legal obligations. The latest version will be published on this page with the date it was last updated.

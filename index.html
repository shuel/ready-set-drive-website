Why Ready Set Drive?

Learning to drive should feel positive, not pressured.

We understand that learning to drive can feel very different for everyone. Some learners are excited to get started, while others may feel nervous or need a little more time to build their confidence.

That's why our lessons are calm, patient and structured around the individual. We focus on helping you understand what you're doing, develop safe driving habits and make steady progress towards becoming a confident, independent driver.
